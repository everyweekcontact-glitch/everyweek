Method

Our approach is simple: observe, define a few useful rules, and execute them every week. We prefer small, transparent systems that teams can maintain.

1 · Discovery Context, goals and constraints. We map current process and responsibilities.
2 · Rules Definitions, thresholds and templates. Everyone knows who does what and when.
3 · Tools We adapt to your stack. Docs, sheets and trackers live together and stay searchable.
4 · Cadence Weekly checkpoints with short notes and honest metrics. Progress compounds.
